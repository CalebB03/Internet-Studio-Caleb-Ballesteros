Welcome to Retro Store Radar!!!

A place where one could search for retro stores in their area, engae with the community, or even submit stores one may have discovered. Here is just a brief overview of what the various pages on the site offer.

Store Showcase Page

We have a store showcase page, which serves as a catalog of every store submitted onto the site. Perfect if you just want to scroll through and see what stores are present instead of using the map.

Community Page

We have a community page as well. Here is a spot to interact with other users anonymously. You can post about any stores you may have discovered, any new retro videogames you may have picked up, or even discuss the weather if you want.

Search Page

We have a search page present on the site. Here you can search for retro stores using the interactable map we have on the page.

Submit Page

Find a retro store not present on the site's database, or a store owner who wants to put their store on the map, well just fill out the submit form on the page, and we'll add your store of choice to the site.

Contact Page

If you encounter any issues, have suggestions, questions, or just want to talk to me (it's okay, you don't have to), then fill out the contact form and we'll look into your inquiries.

About Page

Lastly, we have the About page. This is the oage that contains a brief overview on what the site is capable of, a little bit of information about the creator of the site, and our mission statement in regards to the site.

Well, that just about covers all the bases. Hope you find the site to your liking, and happy retro store hunting!
