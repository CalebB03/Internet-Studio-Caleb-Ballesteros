Six Thinking Hats for my Semester Project

Summaries For Each Hat

White Hat

The conversation was an assessment of the concept and the vision I had for the project. A big question that was brought up was how information on the site was to be gathered, and whether it would be put in by the site owner itself, or have store owners and users contribute to the site. There were also questions about what exactly would be featured within the site itself, and how many little subsections would be in place. Basically, a lot of talk was had about the structure.

Red Hat

The feelings from the discussion were rather positive. The conversation looked at how the project had a feeling of community to it, mainly due to how the users could interact with either each other or the site itself. There were also feelings of potential with the idea, and how there were potential ways for this idea for the project to go. Of course, there were a few feelings of how there could be a few hiccups with the idea. All these were further explored in the other discussions that were had.

Black Hat

A concern that was brought up in regards to the project was that of consent from store owners, primarily when it came to the idea of users being able to post store information for others to see. We discussed how while some store owners may be okay with users helping get the name out there, others may not want to be put on the spot without consent, or at all for that matter. Another talking point was that of an interactive map, like the kind you see from a Google search. While the idea is sound, it was mentioned how, in the context for the project, that it might just be a tad ambitious, especially if we don’t learn how to incorporate something like that.

Yellow Hat

The conversation focused primarily on the benefits. Discussions on how the site could benefit its target demographic were the majority talking points. It was said how it could benefit game collectors who want to find retro stores in their area. It was also mentioned how the site could benefit store owners due to essentially providing free publicity. Finally, a point was made that, due to the community aspect of the site, it could serve to be a meeting place of retro game collecting enthusiasts.

Green Hat

This discussion was all about looking at what I had as my plan, and see what potential avenues could be further explored or new ideas brought to the table. We also thought of any solutions for anything brought up during the black hat discussion. One of these was the idea of store owners consenting to having their store put on the site, and how we could implement a system that would allow the store to contact the site easily or have an alert seeking consent, so that way they could approve a request to appear on the site, or seek out to have their store removed, and maybe have a list of stores who don’t want to appear on the site on hand, and make it so those stores cannot be added without the owners consent.

Blue Hat

This last conversation served as a summary over everything discussed, and talks were had on where we should put our focus on, and what I should possibly consider moving forward with the semester project. We decided that for most of the discussions, we made good strides in what was talked about, and the thoughts and perspectives being shared, but felt that there could be room for some more red hat thinking, since the emotions were positive, but nothing too crazy, where as the other hats had plenty of meat to their discussions.

How This Affects the Project

The discussions helped get an outsider perspective on the project. Instead of bouncing these ideas and thoughts to myself and relying on my judgement, I got to see the idea from someone else’s point of view, and it served to help me get a clearer idea for how I should approach the project. I’ll mostly be sticking to what I was initially going to do, but may look at what I’m putting in the project with a more careful eye. I’ll be looking at the whole thing, seeing what works and running with it, while looking at the areas of concern that were brought up during the discussions, and seeing whether they could be resolved or better left unused in the grand scheme of things. That’s where it stands as of now.
