Reading Questions

Question 1

Some pros for sketching over software for wireframing are things like speed due to not having to worry about finding elements and features, flexibility due to lack of constraints, and limited distractions due to not having to worry about the visual aspects of the wireframe. Some cons with sketching include things like not being able to easily duplicate and transfer your work, a lack of interactivity, and just in general not being visually appealing and potentially even useless if it isn't neat enough.

Question 2

In terms of which style would work best with my project, I think the Mid-fi option would work best. I prefer digital over pen and paper any day, mainly due to the fact that I have not the neatest pencil strokes and hand writing, so I feel like things could get lost in translation. The interactivity that comes with Mid-fi could help get what I'm going for across. While it may be unrefined, it could show where there could be room for improvement and appropriate changes when it comes to the design.
