Design Statement

My goal when designing these webpages was to capture the feeling of a tropical island brochure. This mainly had to do with the fact that my chosen vacation spot, Isle Delfino, is exactly that: A tropical paradise in the middle of the ocean.

For the fonts used, I decided to go with Island Moments and Montserrat fonts for the headers and paragraphs/unordered lists, respectively. Island Moments speaks for itself, given the chosen location, while Montserrat was chosen mainly due to the fact that I thought it paired pretty nicely with Island moments. The font color was based on a tropical color palette I had found online, and it looked pretty good behind the background I had chosen for the webpages, so I went with that particular palette.

The font size was increased from what was initially put in with the type of font generator, mainly to make the text easier to read. Except for the hero image on the index html page, and the map of the island on the contact page, the images on the activities and dining html pages retain a consistent size and are positioned above the text accompanying them. At the time of this project’s creation, we didn’t learn too many ways to animate the buttons, so I ported over the animations we had made for the in-class lesson into the buttons for this project. The CTA Buttons were placed right next to the contact info button, as they are related, although they don't function. As for the button color, I made it a darker shade of orange to contrast with the peach color of the banner, and due to the fact that it stands out a bit more.
