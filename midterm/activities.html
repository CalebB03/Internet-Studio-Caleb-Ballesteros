Actvitites To Do

Casino Delfino

Want something to do while you enjoy yourself in our luxurious hotel? Well, come down to the first floor and enter the wonderful Delfino Casino. Here we have many slot machines to indulge in. We also have a few larger slot machines that provide an extra challenge but provide bigger rewards if you can hit those sevens. We also have a giant puzzle adorning one of our walls, where if you manage to solve it, you get to enjoy a fabulous prize. Even if you don’t feel the slots, you can come in and enjoy refreshments and the atmosphere.


Pinna Park

On an island just a mere boat ride away, it’s none other than Pinna Island, home of the wonderful Pinna Park. Pinna Park is an amusement park filled with loads of activities. You can ride our fast roller coaster for the thrill or use our plastic rockets to pop some ballons while you ride a chance to earn fabulous prizes. Want something more laid back? Then hop on our Yoshi-Go-Round and Clam Cups and have a slow spin. See the heights of the island by getting on to our huge Ferris wheel. Along with all those attractions, we also have a water park, with our huge pirate ships taking center stage above the water.


Fluff Festival

Around this time of year, our lovely Pianta Village celebrates the annual Fluff Festival. This is the time of year when giant dandelions grow and are whisked away by the wind. This is also the time when the sunshine provided by the Shine Sprites is at its strongest. Come and join us for fun games, food, and music as we celebrate the Fluff Festival and give thanks to the precious sunshine that coats our island.
