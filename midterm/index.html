Isle Delfino: The Best Vacation Spot You'll Ever See

What is Isle Delfino?

Isle Delfino is a sun-drenched tropical paradise located in the oceans of the Mushroom Kingdom. It is an island where the sunshine hits the hardest, leading to the best weather an island like this has to offer. The island is also home to many activities to tickle that tourist bug in you. It is home to many luxurious facilities, nice beaches, a fantastic amusement park, and even some delicious seafood to satisfy anyone’s appetite. If you’re looking for a prime vacation spot, you won’t find anything better than Isle Delfino!

Interesting Facts

  • The island is shaped like a Dolphin, which is where the island got its name from.
  • The powerful natural sunlight that beams down on the island is thanks to the powerful Shine Sprites.
  • The island has many unsolved mysteries, like the ancient murals located in Noki Bay.
  • The residents are very unique, as they either reside in shells or have trees atop their heads.
  • If there’s one thing the residents hate, it’s a mess. Be sure to clean up after yourself.
