Domain Name

Summary of Discussion

I brought up these domain names with a good buddy of mine, in order to seek a second opinion. Our discussion began with me describing the site I had planned to make for the course, mainly focusing on the primary function of the site, which was helping users find retro game stores in their area. I then presented the three domain names I had in mind, which were RetroStoreRadar.com, ClassicStoreSearch.com, and RetroStoreHunt.com. Out of the three, he said he felt the first one, RetroStoreRadar.com, worked the best, mainly due to how when he thinks of radars, he thinks of finding something nearby. He also mentioned how he felt that having “Retro” and “Radar” in the name made it easier to remember.

Chosen Domain Name

Out of the three domain names listed above, my favorite would probably be the first one listed, RetroStoreRadar.com. Honestly, I chose it primarily because, out of all three, it sounded the coolest. I liked how it sounded, and after the discussion I had with my buddy, and the points that he raised about it, that only reaffirmed my decision to go with that domain name. I may end up shortening it to RetroRadar, and I may replace the .com, but I haven’t decided on that yet. Regardless, that’s the domain name I’m gonna be rolling with.
